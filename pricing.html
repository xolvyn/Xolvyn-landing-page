Pricing questions

What counts as an active developer?

Anyone who opens a pull request on a connected private repository during a billing month. People who only read the map or view the dashboard — engineering managers, new hires still ramping up — don’t count, so you never pay for them.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Starter and Team include a 14-day free trial on your private repositories. Public repositories are free on every plan, forever.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes. Yearly billing saves 20%. You can switch billing period or change plans at any time, and the change applies from your next billing cycle.

I joined the waitlist. What is founding pricing?

Teams who join the waitlist before general availability get the Team plan at $19 per active developer per month, locked in for life.

Does any plan store or train on my code?

No — on every plan, including Free. Xolvyn shallow-clones your repository, analyzes it in memory, and deletes it immediately. Access is OAuth-only, and your code is never used to train any model.
