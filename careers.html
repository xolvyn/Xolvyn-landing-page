// WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS

Read this before you apply.

Most careers pages are a wall of perks and adjectives. Here's the unvarnished version, because the wrong fit wastes both our time.

01

It's tiny. For now it's you and the founder.

No middle managers, no Jira theater, no "let me loop in five stakeholders." That means you'll learn directly from the founder every day, your work ships fast, and nothing you build gets buried in committee. It also means we figure plenty out together — so questions are expected, never penalized. The smallness is the feature.

02

What month one actually looks like.

Week 1: get to know the existing codebase (you'll literally use Xolvyn to do it), set up your environment, and ship one small, visible fix so we both feel the loop close.

Weeks 2–4: start contributing to the PR-impact bot — helping scope it, building pieces of it, and shipping your work behind a flag, with the founder pairing alongside you. You'll chat with the founder daily and meet early users. No throwaway busywork.

03

What's genuinely hard about it.

You'll touch a lot of different things — frontend polish in the morning, an OAuth quirk after lunch, a VS Code extension API by evening. The work won't always arrive as a perfectly defined ticket; we'll figure out scope together, and asking for help is encouraged, not a black mark. It's a small team, so when something breaks you'll be part of fixing it — but never on your own. If you only feel comfortable with a long, pre-written backlog handed to you, this might not be your speed just yet.

04

The real money & equity math.

We're pre-revenue, in private beta, launching August 2026. We're not going to pretend we can beat a big-tech offer on cash — we can't, and anyone who says otherwise is selling you something.

What's real: an honest, entry-level salary (or a stipend, if you join as an intern) set against your needs and our runway, a meaningful early-equity grant for joining this early, and a comp conversation in plain numbers on the very first real call — not after three rounds of vibes. For a first or second role, the learning curve here is its own kind of pay.
