About Xolvyn

Xolvyn is building the intelligence layer for modern codebases — an AI-powered platform that maps your architecture, audits your security, and ranks every finding by blast radius so teams fix what can actually break things first.

We're a small team of engineers who've felt the pain firsthand: 3-week onboarding, invisible dependencies, security issues discovered at 2am. Xolvyn is what we wished existed at every company we've worked at.
